## Data availability

The data supporting this study are derived from the MIMIC-IV (v3.1) and eICU Collaborative Research Database (v2.0). Both are de-identified, controlled-access datasets available from PhysioNet to credentialed users who meet the respective data-use requirements. The authors cannot redistribute the source data or derived participant-level analytic files. SQL queries and cohort-definition rules used to identify the study populations are available in this repository.
